# Characters

## Main Characters

### Anna Bennett / Anna Park
**Role:** Protagonist
**Age Range:** 20 (Columbia sophomore) → 75 (death)
**Occupation:** Student → Junior Analyst → VP of M&A → SVP → Board Member → Board Chair at Henderson & Associates
**Physical Description:** Naturally striking in college - no makeup, raw and unpolished. By her 40s, becomes polished and intentionally powerful in appearance with professional makeup, expensive suits, Cartier watch, Louboutin heels.
**Key Traits:** 
- Intensely ambitious, driven, calculating
- From working-class background (mother was cashier, stepfather repaired cars in Yonkers)
- Obsessively organized, makes lists constantly
- Tennis player in college (fluid baseline, violent forehand)
- President of Women in Business at Columbia
- Eventually becomes a murderer who kills Jessica Hall
- Emotionally unavailable, prioritizes career over everything
**Arc:** Transforms from hungry, ambitious student to ruthless executive who murders to protect her career, ultimately becoming hollow despite achieving all professional goals
**Death:** Dies at 75 from complications after broken hip (pneumonia, organ failure)

### Andrew Park
**Role:** Anna's husband, artist
**Age Range:** ~22 → 80 (estimated based on marriage at graduation)
**Occupation:** Sculptor and mixed-media artist - creates assemblages in resin, collages of found objects
**Physical Description:** Tall, Asian, good-looking in a natural way. No beard, clean-shaven. A bit sloppy—barefoot, paint on forearms, hair too long, often wearing worn t-shirts. Kind face. Natural, comfortable in his own skin.
**Key Traits:** 
- Gentle, artistic, emotionally available
- Primary caregiver for their children
- Deeply affected by sister Mia's death
- Has emotional/possibly physical affair with Jessica Hall
- Loves Anna but feels invisible in their marriage
**Key Quote:** "I've been waiting for you to come home for seventeen years"
**Death:** Heart attack at ~52, two years before the end of the story
**Remarriage:** Marries Catherine (gallery owner) after divorce

### Jessica Hall
**Role:** Junior Project Manager, catalyst for Anna's descent
**Age:** 29 at death
**Occupation:** Junior Project Manager at Henderson & Associates
**Physical Description:** Warm smile, professional appearance
**Key Traits:** 
- Competent, kind, remembers birthdays
- Has emotional affair with Andrew after Mia's death
- Manipulated by Jane into connecting with Andrew
- Actually innocent of sabotaging Anna
**Fate:** Murdered by Anna (staged as suicide by hanging) on Friday night at her apartment (128 Perry Street, 4C, West Village)
**Death Date:** Specific Friday, found the following Wednesday
**Legacy:** Andrew never stopped loving her; her death haunts Anna but she feels nothing

### Jane van Moore
**Role:** Mentor turned antagonist/mastermind
**Age:** ~45 → 63 (released from prison) → 80+ (estimated)
**Occupation:** Board member at Henderson & Associates, Anna's mentor for 15 years
**Physical Description:** Immaculate, knife-sharp blazer, glossy hair
**Key Traits:** 
- Brilliant, manipulative, psychopathic
- Never married, no children - sacrificed everything for career
- Lives in Tribeca penthouse
- Orchestrates multi-year campaign to destroy Anna
- Secretly jealous that Anna was building career AND family
**Major Crimes:** 
- Murdered Mia Park (Andrew's sister) via fentanyl overdose
- Orchestrated sabotage campaign against Anna (phantom emails, corrupted files, calendar glitches)
- Recruited Jason Whitmore to destabilize Anna
- Manipulated Jessica into affair with Andrew
- Possibly killed Jessica to frame Anna
**Fate:** Convicted of Mia's murder, serves 18 years in prison, finds religion, released at 63, tries to warn Anna about her soul
**Key Quote:** "I created you... I made you into exactly what I wanted you to be."

### Claire
**Role:** Anna's best friend from Columbia
**Age:** Same age as Anna (20 → 75+)
**Occupation:** Finance major → Mid-level manager at a small pharma company (regulatory affairs, product lifecycle)
**Physical Description:** Natural blonde hair in practical bob, warm laugh
**Key Traits:** 
- Loyal friend who never abandons Anna despite everything
- The moral conscience of the story
- Has "comfortable" balanced life - stable marriage, becomes grandmother
- Chosen family and balance over pure ambition
- Works reasonable hours at a modest firm, prioritized life over career climb
**Key Role:** Serves as foil to Anna - shows what Anna could have been
**Arc:** Remains Anna's friend through everything, ultimately present at Anna's deathbed

### Jason Whitmore
**Role:** Anna's college ex-boyfriend, later colleague
**Age Range:** ~22 (Columbia) → ~62 (when story ends)
**Occupation:** Finance major at Columbia → Goldman → SVP of Private Equity at Henderson & Associates
**Physical Description:** 
- At Columbia: Expensive cologne, confident, legacy wealth (watch costs more than stepfather's car)
- At 42 (when he joins Henderson): Hair has gone darker—from brown to deep, rich dark brown, thick and well-groomed. Beard, also dark, with just the right amount of grey threaded through both hair and beard—distinguished, sexy. Seems taller (presence), more attractive than in college. Expensive suits, same confident smile
**Background:** Old money, legacy at Columbia, Wharton MBA
**Relationship with Anna:** 
- Dated at Columbia, planned future together including moving in together
- Anna broke up with him senior spring because he wanted to control her life and career
- Jason's version: Anna was controlling, hacked his email, tried to sabotage his London internship
**Return:** Recruited to Henderson by mysterious board member (Jane) to destabilize Anna, appears at Andrew's gallery
**Key Trait:** More sympathetic than Anna initially believes - actually didn't know Anna worked at Henderson when he took the job

## Family Members

### Margaret Bennett
**Role:** Anna's mother
**Occupation:** Cashier
**Key Traits:** Working-class, took unpaid day off for Anna's Columbia graduation, wore dress bought on sale, incredibly proud of Anna
**Key Scene:** Screamed Anna's name at graduation "like she'd won the lottery"

### Anna's Stepfather
**Occupation:** Car mechanic in garage that smelled like oil and sun-baked rubber
**Key Trait:** Fixed a Lexus and "thinks he's a surgeon"

### Sophie Park
**Role:** Anna and Andrew's daughter
**Age Range:** 0 (born ~2013) → 30+
**Occupation:** Surgeon
**Key Traits:** 
- Sharp, self-contained, perceptive
- Becomes emotionally distant from Anna
- Studies medicine - "as far from her mother's world as possible"
- Eventually marries and has children (Anna's grandchildren)
- Sees Anna clearly: "You have everything you wanted and you're still empty"

### Jake Park
**Role:** Anna and Andrew's son  
**Age:** 0 (born ~2015) → 24+
**Occupation:** Teaching art (following father's path)
**Key Traits:** 
- Scraped knees and boundless energy as child
- Looks like Andrew, has Andrew's quiet kindness
- Becomes distant from Anna
- Reveals Andrew still cried about Jessica

### Mia Park
**Role:** Andrew's sister, murder victim
**Age:** 34 at death
**Occupation:** Painted sets for off-Broadway shows
**Key Traits:** Chaotic, vivid energy, infectious laugh, debated with Sophie, stole Jake's fries
**Addiction:** Long history of addiction, multiple relapses
**Death:** Murdered by Jane van Moore via intentionally lethal fentanyl dose (February 14) to destabilize Andrew and Anna's marriage
**Impact:** Her death was the catalyst that made Andrew vulnerable to Jessica's emotional support

### Catherine
**Role:** Andrew's second wife
**Occupation:** Gallery owner
**Key Traits:** Kind, interesting, good with Sophie and Jake, gracious
**Marriage:** Marries Andrew ~1 year after divorce from Anna, small ceremony with Sophie and Jake as witnesses
**Death:** Dies from cancer ~2 years after Andrew's death
**Key Quote to Anna at Andrew's funeral:** "He never stopped loving you... but I saw in his face his heart was not with me [it was with Jessica]"
