# Novel Structure Plan

## **SUMMARY OF WRITTEN CHAPTERS (Chapters 1-9)**

### **Chapter 1: Columbia, Year Two (Fall 2003)**
- Hanna Bennett, sophomore at Columbia: ambitious finance major from working-class Yonkers family
- President of Women in Business, varsity tennis player, driven by desire to support her mother (cashier) and stepfather (mechanic)
- Best friend Claire (also finance major), mentor Jane van Moore (3 years older, works at Henderson & Associates in M&A)
- Dating Jason Whitmore: wealthy legacy student who treats Hanna well but subtly tries to control/manage her
- Hanna is naturally striking—no makeup, effortless beauty—driven by lists and discipline
- Gets first-round interview at Henderson & Associates

### **Chapter 2: The Real World (2003)**
- Jane reveals she's pregnant and getting an abortion on Saturday to protect her career
- Shows Hanna the harsh reality: successful career means loneliness, sacrifice, impossible choices
- Jane's advice: "Don't get pregnant. Don't let men control your calendar. Say no to the wrong things faster."
- Jason offers to get Hanna's mother a "better job" through his connections, wants to "help" Hanna relax and delegate
- Hanna feels suffocated by his well-meaning control but stays with him
- Hanna works the Women in Business fundraiser, Jason shows possessive behavior
- Jane's Saturday appointment happens off-screen—by Monday she's back at work

### **Chapter 3: The Year Everything Shifted (Senior Year 2006)**
- Senior spring: Hanna gets Henderson & Associates offer (Junior Analyst, M&A)
- Jason gets Goldman offer (via family connections), Claire gets med-tech strategy role
- Jason pushes Hanna to move in together, talks about marriage, children, Hanna "stepping back" after a few years at Henderson
- **THE BREAKUP:** Hanna finally leaves Jason after realizing he's been planning her life without asking her—"I have always felt that he wanted to boss me around. I just couldn't take it anymore."
- Jane and Claire support Hanna through the breakup
- Graduation: Hanna's mother and stepfather attend (mother took unpaid day off, wore sale dress)
- Hanna chooses herself and her career over Jason's controlled vision of their future

### **Chapter 4: Twenty Years Later (Present Day)**
- Hanna is 42, VP of M&A at Henderson & Associates, eyeing SVP promotion
- Married to Steve Park (artist/sculptor) for 17 years, kids Sophie (14) and Jake (8)
- Lives in Upper West Side apartment, polished professional appearance (complete transformation from natural college look)
- Jane van Moore now sits on the board—powerful, cold, still Hanna's champion
- SVP meeting scheduled for next week
- Hanna's marriage functional but distant; Steve handles home life while she works constantly

### **Chapter 5: Strange Things**
- Metallic-tasting coffee left anonymously at reception for Hanna
- Mysterious lemon tart with note "For the future SVP. Sweet things ahead" appears on her desk
- Unsent email draft appears on Hanna's computer (she didn't write it) addressed to Tom Harrison
- Jessica Hall introduced: new junior project manager (3 months on team), too perfect, brings Hanna coffee with exact order
- Analysts gossip: **Jason Whitmore joining as SVP of Private Equity**—Columbia alum, Wharton MBA, old money, "referenced by someone on the board"
- Unknown text to Hanna: *"Congrats in advance"*
- Hanna realizes her college ex is coming to work in her building

### **Chapter 6: The Sister**
- Steve's sister Mia dies of fentanyl overdose at 34 (had history of addiction)
- Steve devastated; Hanna manages funeral logistics but can't emotionally connect
- Hanna's SVP meeting postponed indefinitely by Tom due to "Archer deal crisis"
- Jessica sends Steve condolence note, then starts texting him about Mia and his art
- Jessica buys one of Steve's pieces, visits his studio, says she "gets" his grief work
- **Sabotage escalates:** phantom emails sent from Hanna's account at 2am, corrupted presentations, fake client calls, office plant poisoned
- Steve tells Hanna that Jessica "listens" about Mia in a way Hanna doesn't
- Hanna confronts Jessica professionally, asks her to respect boundaries with Steve
- Hanna and Steve fight: "You're making this about you... You can't sit with me for five minutes without checking your email"
- Steve leaves angry; Hanna works late, feels marriage cracking
- Fake hospital call claiming Hanna's mother had a fall (mother was fine, never admitted)

### **Chapter 7: Fractures**
- SVP promotion still not rescheduled three weeks after Mia's funeral; Tom keeps saying "when Archer stabilizes" (Archer already stable)
- Hanna feels nothing—"flat hum of another day"
- Sabotage continues: files renamed, wrong client logos, voicemails with dead air
- IT says no breach, suggests "user error"—Hanna starting to look disorganized
- Steve becomes ghost at home: paints until midnight, barely speaks
- Jessica still texting Steve about gallery connections
- Hanna discovers Steve still communicating with Jessica despite her asking Jessica to give him space
- Steve defends it: "She's helping my career. She asks about Mia. She doesn't treat my grief like something I need to hurry up and get over."
- Hanna and Steve fight again; Steve tells her to "Get out"
- Midnight text from unknown number: *"You're losing him"* (then vanishes from phone)
- **CRUCIAL MOMENT:** Hanna checks Steve's phone while he's asleep—sees innocent texts about gallery contacts and grief
- **Hanna concludes:** Steve wasn't cheating, Jessica was just helping professionally and being kind about Mia—"she'd been so paranoid... that she'd turned genuine support into something sinister"
- Hanna decides to "do better" and stop seeing threats, but gets distracted by work crisis and forgets

### **Chapter 8: The Unraveling**
- Hanna stops sleeping (fragments of 1-hour pieces)
- **Sabotage escalates dramatically:**
  - Presentation deck corrupted (every third slide blank) right before client meeting
  - Corrupted file sent from Hanna's email at 2:47 AM (she was asleep)
  - Fake calendar invite to "Meridian Partners" call makes her miss actual Sanderson meeting
  - Email to major client contains decimal point error ($45M instead of $4.5M)
  - Files rename themselves seconds after saving
  - Access badge stops working for executive floor
  - Midnight calls to office phone with just breathing
- Tom expresses concern about Hanna's "focus"—IT found no breach, suggests Hanna is the problem
- Marcus (Hanna's right hand) starts appearing in meetings she normally leads—sends message "She needs supervision"
- Jessica beloved by team: "kind," "wonderful," brings bagels, helps junior analysts
- Hanna starts suspecting Marcus (he has access, would benefit from her failure)
- Hanna buys cash notebook at Target to document everything by hand
- Claire calls worried, suggests therapy—Hanna defensive
- **Weekend shift:** Hanna takes time off, attends Jake's soccer game, helps Sophie with homework, tries to be present
- Hanna sets up tracking: email forwarding rule to private Gmail, laptop tracking software
- Watches Marcus lingering near her office after hours
- Fake calendar invite: "Henderson Leadership Offsite—You've Been Removed" (Tom didn't send it)
- Tom suggests "outside help" or "other resources" (therapy, medical leave)

### **Chapter 9: The Arrival**
- Email announcement: **Jason Whitmore officially joins as SVP, Private Equity**
- Jane immediately texts Hanna: *"ARE YOU OKAY??? Call me."*
- Hanna sees Jason in elevator—20 years have changed him: dark brown hair with grey, beard, more attractive, expensive suit, same confidence
- Jason suggests coffee to "catch up"—Hanna declines
- Jane meets Hanna for lunch: "If he tries anything, you tell me. He doesn't get to make you uncomfortable in your own building."
- Steve vague about evening plans: "meeting someone interested in grief series" at Manuel's gallery
- Unknown text to Hanna: *"He's not where he said he'd be"*
- Hanna checks Steve's location on Find My Phone—he IS at Manuel's gallery where he said he'd be (text was lying, trying to manipulate her)
- **New calendar invite appears:** VP Leadership Lunch Wednesday 12:30pm to welcome Jason (Tom scheduled it, doesn't know about Hanna/Jason history)
- Hanna can't decline without looking suspicious—trapped into pretending she and Jason are strangers
- Hanna sits alone in dark apartment documenting in her notebook:
  - **People who could access accounts:** IT, someone with passwords, physical access to devices, Steve?
  - **People who benefit from failure:** Marcus (wants SVP), Jessica (wants what?), Jason (revenge? to unsettle?)
- Steve comes home at 10:40pm from gallery meeting (exactly where he said he'd be)
- Hanna writes: *"Anonymous text tried to make me think Steve was lying. He wasn't. Someone is watching us. Trying to destabilize me."*
- Final note: *"Who benefits from making me paranoid? Who wants me to doubt Steve? Who wants me to look unstable?"*


**Chapter 10: Broken**
- Starts with Wednesday with the executive lunch for the VP's to meet Jason
- Lunch is tense, Hanna seems nervous around him. Jason tries to talk to Hanna and tell her not to tell about them to anyone.
- Next day Steve goes out at night. Hanna keeps following Steve digitally (GPS tracking) she had already done it last chapter
- **NEW EVIDENCE emerges:** Steve's location pings don't match his alibis, it suggests he is near Jessica's house
- IT suggests someone with "physical access to devices" (home or office)
- **ESCALATION:** Hanna finds evidence pointing to affair
- Once Steve gets back they have a fight about it
- Steve leaves after confrontation about tracking
- Hanna realises that Jessica won't stop talking to Steve, and Steve does not seem interested in stop talking to her either. A broken marrige/divorce is the last thing she needs right now, it would distract her from her goasl, plus, a divorce looks bad to the board. 
- Hanna comes up with a motive for Jessica to do the sabbotage : *When did the sabotage start? Before or after Jessica met Steve? Could Jessica have been behind the sabotage just to ensure I would be emotionally unavailable to my husband?*

**Chapter 11: TBD**
- Hanna obsessively working and documenting
- Meeting scheduled with Tom/Jessica (turns out to be fake)
- **Café confrontation:** Steve and Hanna meet
- Steve admits **"emotional affair"** - says Jessica listens, makes him feel seen
- He won't confirm physical affair but admits closeness
- Hanna tracks Steve to Perry Street (Jessica's building)
- Hanna receives text: *"He's mine now"*
- Phantom email sent from her account to Tom about "taking leave"

**Chapter 12: Breaking Points**
- Tom calls Hanna in about the "leave" email
- Tom suggests Hanna is "struggling" - considering forced leave
- Jessica brings Hanna tea (Hanna doesn't drink it)
- Hanna confronts Jessica about knowing her address and Jessica seems worried and affraid (she is not confrontational though and just tries to please Hanna)
- **Hanna goes to Jessica's building, listens at door, hears conversation** She understands Steve and Jessica are getting along, but still no hard evidence of physical love.
- Hanna sees Steve leaving Jessica's appartment, get a cab close to the river sitting there by himself and crying.

**Chapter 13: Parallel Lives**
- Steve comes for clothes, Hanna confronts him
- **Steve confirms relationship with Jessica** (emotional/possibly physical)
- Uses "Ben" as cover story
- Jason corners Hanna in parking garage, reveals unusual recruitment and asks her if she had anything to do with it since he did not know she was working there.
- Hanna is shocked about what Jason is suggesting, but he geniunly seems to want to make peace. She denies it and just asks for distance.
- Claire calls, plans to visit to help Hanna for the weekend, given Steve is gone to get is shit together at Ben's.
- Overall Hanna seems to be losing her mind, in here we take a brief momment to summarize to the user everything she is jugling in her brain
- Hanna decides to start therapy, she has booked it, but she wont actually go (read chapter 14) 

**Chapter 14: The Gallery**
- Steve's gallery show
- Jane has dinner with Hanna, warns about "looking unstable"
- Hanna receives photo of Steve & Jessica together at gallery, looking happy
- Hanna goes to gallery, watches from outside
- **SEES JASON THERE** talking to Steve and Jessica
- **Conspiracy theory forms:** Jessica/Steve/Jason all connected
- Hanna creates timeline theory: *"Jessica orchestrated everything"*
- Makes calendar event: *"8pm—Resolution"*

**Chapter 15: Preparation**
- Hanna wakes with clarity, dresses for action
- Leaves work early
- Dinner with Sophie and Jake (says goodbye without them knowing)
- Tells kids and Clair she's meeting her therapist at 7pm, but the appointment is only at 8pm, this will buy her 1 hour to do what we reveal in chapter 17
- But Hanna is going to Jessica's apartment very determined to confront her, she knows she will be there because early that day she said she would be there by herself working hard on the client case Hanna thinks she is corrupting (even thought it is friday). Note in this chapter Jessica does not mention her friday night to Jane directly, Hanna is just remembering this
- During the drive there Hanna is piecing the story of how she is the one sabotaging her, she will confront her with all the evidence she has got

### **ACT III: THE MURDER (Chapters 16-18)**

**Chapter 16: Parallel Truths**

**8:15 PM — Jessica's Apartment:**
- Hanna confronts Jessica about affair and sabotage
- Jessica admits affair but denies sabotage
- Hanna puts on gloves
- Jessica fights back
- Hanna restrains her with duct tape
- **Suffocates Jessica with tape**
- Takes 4 minutes
- Hanna cleans scene, stages body, removes evidence

**8:15 PM — The Bar (PARALLEL NARRATIVE):**
- Steve at bar after Jessica had not show up to the movies the had planed to see, he is also super sad Jessica could not come
- Jason Whitmore approaches him, what a coincidence, they had jsut met the previous night in Steve's gallery show
- Jason reveals he's Hanna's ex from Columbia
- **Jason tells twisted version of their breakup** - claims Hanna was controlling, obsessive, hacked his accounts, tried to sabotage his internships and was pissed when he got better grades than her
- Makes Steve doubt Hanna, but overall Jason shows his respects for the family and says he hopes they can all get along ( the seed of mistrust has, however, been planted in Steve's mind)

**8:45 PM — Jessica's Apartment:**
- Hanna finishes cleanup, stages suicide scene by hanging, writes a suicide note talking about a deep depression and a feeling of guilt for wanting an affair with her bosses husband
- Leaves via back entrance
- Disposes of rental car in NJ, Ubers back to Midtown
- Home by 11pm, showers, goes to bed
- Feels nothing, maybe because she herself is still in shock

**Chapter 17: The alibi**
- At 7pm Hanna was in a bad part of town, not very far from the therapy's office, but at least 10 minutes by car.
- The plan was simple: find a prostitute that looked like her, give instructions to go to the psychiatrist and play Hanna. Simple.
- She finds a prostitute that looks like her, asks how much. She hooker says 200 dollars per hour, Hanna says "get in".
- "Where are we going" says the hooker.
- "You are gonna be an actress for a night"
- At first the hooker did not like the idea, asked to get out, though Hanna was up for no good, maybe harm her. 
- Hanna offers 600 dollars for 2 hours (1 hour and a half for the therapist (first appointment is longer) and anotehr 30 minutes to wait for Hanna) and explains she needs a solid alibi because she will cheat on her husband.
- Complains, very convincivle, that her husband abuses her and is super controlling, but that her ex is in town and that she loves him and wants to meet him, maybe just for dinner, but maybe for sex as well. 
- The hooker feels sorry for Hanna, goes along with the idea and goes to the therapy to play Hanna. Hanna trains her for like 10 minutes to sound like her, professional, the hooker was doing very well at it.
- After Hanna comits murder and gets home she texts the therapist asking how the therapist saying that she does not want to do therapy anymore, that it is not for her. But what she really wants is indirect feedback from the therapist, to tell her how the session went with the hooker. 
- The therapist replies saying she is very sorry to hear that and that she felt Hanna really open up to her to way in a cordial way. Perfect

**Chapter 18: After**
- A week later, after not showing up to work for 5 days, the police find jessica still hanging in her apartment
- The police goe sthrough Jessica's phone and decides to question both Steve and Hanna
- Even though the police finds Hanna to be suspicious they end up letting her go, there is no evidence of foul play (Hanna had even cleaned Jessica's nails to avoid DNA) 
- Jessica's funeral - Hanna attends, performs grief perfectly
- Steve moves back in, he was in fact staying at Ben's but he could not live there forever
- **Sabotage stops** (confirms Hanna's suspicions that Jessica was behind it)
- Steve starts sleeping with door locked, checking food, he does not believe Jessica would have killed herself

### **ACT IV: THE AFTERMATH & REVELATION (Chapters 18-26)**

**Chapter 19: False Peace**
- 3 weeks later, life returns to "normal"
- Hanna works, Steve lives like a ghost, barely speaking
- Sophie asks if they're getting divorced, but Hanna reasures her, they have always had the perfect life, it would not stop now
- Hanna returns Jessica's office belongings to her mother
- Mrs. Hall is very friendly to Hanna
- Hanna promoted to full SVP
- Tom mentions board seat possibility in "a year or two", Hanna's great goal. Her life was settling in just like she had planed.

**Chapter 20: Suspicion**
- **WEEK six: Sabotage resumes** (phantom email about resigning)
- Hanna realizes: Jessica was innocent, wasn't behind sabotage
- **Hanna understands she killed the wrong person**
- Sabotage continues: fake resignation announcements, access badge deactivated, files deleted
- Hanna goes through suspect list again, but finds no one. Starts to belive that she is going insane
- Hanna calls Clair and asks her for comapny, she needs her girlfriend in a time of crisis.
- Hanna still does not suspect anyone in particular.

**Chapter 21: The Coffee**
- Claire visits Hanna, she has always been so kind to her
- Hanna starts talking about the sabotagge and how she does not know who is doing it to he.
- Hanna admits she though Jessica was behind the sabotage, but now that Jessica "comited suicide" she does not know who could do it. She admits Jessica had an affair with Steve.
- Claire: *"Think, who else had all of the accesses needed to sabotage you?"*
- Hanna : "I honestly dont know anymore"
- Claire : Suggests stress-induced memory issues, self-sabotage. Maybe this is a structural problem in your firm. Just a few months ago Jane told me she was having similar issues.
- Hanna: What ? What issues ? How come she never told me, she knows I am going through this.
- Claire is vague, but says that a few months ago Jane went to her crying about ... she dismisses it. Says it's nothing, that Jane asked her not to share with Anyone. 
- Hanna presses Claire
- Claire confesses that Jane's issue it was work related, that she seemed to be on the brink of collapse, especially because she was not even that close to Claire, she just had no one to talk to. She adimited that the rest of the board was thinking about letting her go from the board because she had been sleeping with analysts for years, and people were starting to gossip. The fact that she never married does not provide the best image, and the gossips about her sex live in the office were the last straw for the board. 
- Hanna is bafled, but says nothing directly to Claire, keeps her suspicions secret to herself.
- **Focuses on Jane** - board access, system privileges, was at Mia's funeral reception
- Checks timeline: all major incidents coincide with Jane being in office
- Hanna calls both Jane and Claire and books a lunch with both of them for Sunday. She did not want to book it just with Jane not to be too out of the blue. She wants to analyse Jane from up close asap, but needs it to sounds natural.

**Chapter 22: The Lunch**
- The 2 woman meet at the restaurant for lunch
- **Jane deliberately insults Claire** - calls her career "modest," "middle management," "comfortable life"
- Drives wedge between Hanna and Claire
- Claire leaves angry
- Jane tells Hanna: *"Claire will always hold you back. You need to prioritize people who understand your trajectory."*
- Reveals she never married, had kids, focused entirely on career: *"Was it lonely? Sometimes. Was it worth it? Absolutely."*
- Hanna texts Claire apology, Claire responds: *"I know Jane is your boss, but still, she is so mean, please make sure not to invite me when she is around."*
- Hanna decides to break into Jane's apartment for proof

**Chapter 23: The Break-In**
- Hanna watches Jane's schedule (gym every Wednesday, 6 AM, exactly 90 minutes)
- Wednesday 6:05 AM: Hanna enters Jane's building with fake delivery
- Picks locks to apartment
- Finds Jane's office with locked drawers and laptop
- **DISCOVERS PERSONNEL FILE on Hanna** - 15 years of notes, clinical observations, controlling documentation
- Finds USB drive labeled "Backup Systems" with keylogging software, remote access tools
- **FINDS FOLDER: "AP Project"**
- **THE TRUTH:**
  - Timeline showing Jane planned everything
  - Jane recruited Jason to destabilize Hanna
  - Jane identified Jessica as potential affair target
  - **Chat logs: Jane encouraged Jessica to reach out to Steve**
  - **Computer messages in a shady website that suggest a deal with a drug dealer: Cash purchase of fentanyl, 2 months before Mia died**
  - **Jane's notes proving she murdered Mia deliberately** to destabilize Steve/Hanna's marriage
- Hanna photographs everything, copies USB to phone
- Escapes at 7:20 AM (Jane due back at 7:35)
- Calls Claire: *"Jane killed Mia. She orchestrated everything. I have proof."*

**Chapter 24: The Plan**
- Hanna and Steve meet, she shows him evidence
- Steve confirms: *"She deliberately... killed my sister?"*
- Steve confronts Hanna : " But why did you break-in in the first place"
- Hanna tells Steve all about the sabotagge
- They can't go to police directly (break-in is illegal)
- **Plan: Anonymous delivery** of evidence, claim it was sent to them by whistleblower
- They prepare clean copies (no fingerprints, no metadata)
- Print at different libraries, use generic envelopes
- Steve asks: *"Do you think she could have killed Jessica to frame you?"* Hanna: *"I could not find evidence, but most likely"*
- They drop envelope at 6th Precinct at 5 AM Wednesday
- Detective Morris calls Hanna: anonymous tip received about Jane van Moore

**Chapter 25: Unraveling**
- **Jane arrested Friday morning** - murder charges for Mia's death
- Tom calls Hanna: *"Jane has been destroying your life this whole time?"*
- Media circus: *Henderson Board Member Arrested for Murder*, Tom does not like this, not one bit
- Hanna treated as victim at work
- Three weeks later: given Hanna as been promoted a couple of months now and that there is a vacancie in the board, everything seems set to have Hanna reaching for the sky.
- Hanna wins everything she wanted
- But realizes cost: Jessica dead (innocent), Mia dead, marriage shattered (Steve is still distant, clearly he still has feelings for Jessica), soul hollowed out


**Chapter 26: Jane's Defense**
- Preliminary hearing, Jane arrives composed
- Jane's eyes find Hanna's across courtroom - **smiles**
- Jane's lawyer approaches: Jane wants to speak privately
- **Jail meeting:** Jane: *"I created you. Fifteen years of mentorship. I showed you what power really costs."*
- Jane admits she was always afraid Hanna would take her place because her life was more stable, while herself was always having casual one night stands with people from the office
- **Jane tells Hanna she is quite sure she killed Jessica:** *"You murdered an innocent woman. That's on you."* Why does she believe this ? she does not know for sure, but she knows that she had been trying to get Hanna to believe Jessica was the one sabotaging her, and all of the sudden a woman that is super happy dies of suicide ? She tries to get Hanna to confess, but Hanna does not. This is a very tense moment of conversation. 
- Jane: *"I want you to know—you're just like me. The only difference is I owned it."*
- Jane asks about the control Hanna felt suffocating Jessica
- Jane explains: *"Even though I can't have you locked up, watching you become who you really are is more satisfying than revenge."*
- **Trial:** Jury convicts Jane, 15 years to life
- As Jane is led away, she mouths: **"Welcome to the board."**

### **ACT V: THE HOLLOW VICTORY (Chapters 27-31)**

**Chapter 27: Aftermath**

- Steve asks: *"Was it worth it? Everything you sacrificed?"*

- Hanna turns 60 in an empty penthouse

- Sophie/Jake send gifts, don't attend

- So Hanna is now 60, no family, no love, alone. What could she want, out of pure habit she is aiming for the board chair. She doesn't even know why anymore, it is just blind ambition.
- Jason had been a part of the board for 8 years now. And Jane overhears the board set guy saying to another board member "I'm close to retring now, and I was thinking about Jason for my place, I think he's the right guy, you ont take it personally right ? " - "never, I'm close to retiring myself, but what about Hanna, she's been working ferociously" - "Hanna ?! No, I would n't let a woman close to the seat, not in my lifetime"
- Hanna thinks to herself that it's time to do everything she learned from Jane, except being caught
- Leave the end open for the reader to assume that Hanna will now do to Jason what Jane had donne to her all those years ago.
